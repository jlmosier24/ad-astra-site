About Ad Astra Active

Ad Astra Active organizes field trips for our homeschool co-op's member families — hands-on history, science, and nature outings a few times each season.

Trips are open to member families. If you're not yet part of the co-op, reach out through the Contact page to learn more about joining.

How to Register
1
Browse upcoming trips
Scroll the trip list on the homepage to see what's coming up, including dates and pricing.
2
Pick a trip and hit Register
Select the trip you'd like to join and click Register to open the sign-up form.
3
Enter your member email
We check your email against our member list — no account or password needed.
4
Fill in your details and confirm
Add how many adults and kids are coming, then submit — you're registered.
5
Check your inbox
A confirmation email lands right away with your registration details.
Not a member yet? Ad Astra Active trips are for co-op member families. If you'd like to learn more about joining, get in touch through our Contact page.
